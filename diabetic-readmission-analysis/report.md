# Diabetic Patients Readmission Analysis Report

## Dataset Overview
- Total patients: 101766
- Total columns: 50

## Key Findings

1. **Readmission Rate**
   - Patients NOT readmitted (NO): 54,864 (53.91%)
   - Patients readmitted >30 days (>30): 35,545 (34.93%)
   - Patients readmitted <30 days (<30): 11,357 (11.16%)

2. **Age Distribution**
   - Most patients are in age group: 70-80
   - Total patients analyzed: 101,766

## Conclusion

From the analysis, I found that 46.09% of diabetic patients were readmitted (either within <30 days or >30 days). 

Readmissions within <30 days are 11.16%, which are urgent readmissions. Readmissions after >30 days are 34.93%, suggesting chronic condition management issues.

Hospitals should focus on post-discharge care for diabetic patients, especially those aged 70-80, to reduce readmission rates.

Efficient chronic condition management is achieved by using connected digital tools for continuous home tracking and care coordinators to unify communication between all specialists. These solutions prevent medical emergencies by catching complications early and removing conflicting treatments.
